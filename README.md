# 🚀 Welcome to Geek Room ADGIPS

Welcome to the official GitHub Organization of **Geek Room - ADGIPS Chapter**!  
We are a community of student developers, open-source enthusiasts, and tech creators building innovative solutions and fostering a collaborative learning culture.

---

## 🎯 What We Do
- 🛠️ **Open Source Projects:** Building impactful applications and internal tools.
- 🏆 **Hackathons & Competitions:** Hosting and participating in inter-college tech events.
- 💡 **Workshops & Bootcamps:** Hands-on sessions on Web, AI/ML, Cyber Security, App Dev, and Cloud.
- 🤝 **Mentorship:** Connecting students with experienced seniors, leads, and alumni.
